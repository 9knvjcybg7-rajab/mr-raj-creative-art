How to enrol.

Choose your course, send a quick request on WhatsApp, then pay securely with M-Pesa.

  1. Choose your course. Pick Illustrator, Photoshop, or Procreate below.
  2. Send your request. The form opens WhatsApp with your details ready. Press send to complete your request.
  3. Pay with M-Pesa. Use the Paybill details below. The fee is KSh 2,500 per course.
  4. Confirm your payment. Send your M-Pesa confirmation message on WhatsApp so we can confirm your place and next steps.
